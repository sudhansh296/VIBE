Safety center

Your safety comes first. Here are the tools and tips to stay safe on VIBE.

Your tools

Stay safe

Never share your OTP or UPI PIN
VIBE staff will never ask for them.
Don't pay outside VIBE
If someone asks for money on UPI or another app, it is a scam. Report them.
Keep your number private
Stay on VIBE calls. Do not give phone numbers or social ids to people you just met.
Be careful meeting anyone
We do not recommend meeting people from the app. If you do, tell a friend and choose a public place.
Report anything that feels wrong
Reports are private and reviewed by our team, usually within 24 hours.
In danger? Call your local emergency number first. For women's helpline in India dial 181.
