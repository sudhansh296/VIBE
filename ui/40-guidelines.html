Community guidelines

VIBE is for friendly, respectful conversations between people who are 18 or older. These rules apply to everyone: users and hosts.

Please do

✓Be kind and respect the other person's no.
✓Use your own real photos and your real age.
✓Keep calls and payments inside VIBE.
✓Block and report anything that feels wrong.

Never do

✕Nudity or sexual content, in photos, videos, calls or chat.
✕Abuse, threats or hate against anyone.
✕Asking for money, phone numbers, UPI or social ids outside VIBE.
✕Fake profiles, stolen photos, scams or spam.
✕Anyone under 18. We remove these accounts.
✕Recording or sharing a call or someone's photo without their consent.

What happens if rules are broken

1
Warning
The content is removed and you get a message
2
Temporary ban
You can't use calls, chat or your host status for some days
3
Permanent ban
The account is closed. Serious cases skip straight to this. Unpaid earnings may be held

The rules and how we act on them are managed by VIBE and may be updated.
