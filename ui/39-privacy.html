Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Version 1.0

✓We never sell your personal data.
✓Your selfie and ID are seen only by the VIBE verification team.
✓You can delete your account and data from Settings.

This is a summary page. The full, legally reviewed policy is managed by VIBE and can be updated at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: phone number, name, date of birth, gender, city, languages, photos and videos you add.
  • For hosts: selfie, ID and PAN, and UPI or bank details for payouts.
  • Activity: calls, messages, gifts, coin and payment history, visitors and statuses.
  • Device information, crash logs and app usage to keep the app working and safe.

Why we use it

  • To run calls, chat, wallet, payouts and gifts.
  • To verify age and identity, stop fraud and keep people safe.
  • To review reports and enforce the Community guidelines.
  • To send you notifications you asked for.

Camera, microphone and storage

Camera and microphone are used only for calls, your selfie check, videos and photos you choose to add. Calls are not recorded by VIBE.

Who sees your information

Other users see your name, age, city, photos, status and online state according to your privacy settings. We share data only with services that help us run VIBE, such as payment, SMS and cloud providers, or when the law requires.

Your choices

  • Hide your online status and visitor trail in Settings.
  • Block and report anyone.
  • Edit your profile or delete your account at any time.

How long we keep data

We keep data while your account is active. After you delete it we remove or anonymise your data, except records we must keep for payments, tax, safety or the law.

Contact

For privacy questions open Help & support in Settings.
